Questions, answered

How does InsurePro make money if quotes are free?

When you choose to buy a policy through us, the carrier pays us a standard commission that's already baked into the premium you'd pay anywhere else. You never pay us a fee, and commissions never change your price or our recommendations.

Will comparing quotes affect my credit score?

No. Browsing and running quotes uses a soft inquiry only. A hard credit check happens later — and only in states where insurers are permitted to use credit as a rating factor — right before you purchase.

How quickly can I get proof of insurance?

Immediately. The moment your payment is approved, your ID cards and declarations page appear in your dashboard and land in your email. Digital cards are accepted by police and lenders in all 50 states.

Can I bundle policies from different carriers?

Yes. Bundle discounts usually require policies with the same carrier, so we first show you matched bundles — and if another carrier beats the bundled price, we'll show you that too.

What if I already have insurance elsewhere?

We'll pull your current coverage details, flag gaps and over-insured areas, and calculate your cancellation date so you never pay for overlapping days. Most switches take under 10 minutes.

How do I file a claim?

Sign in, pick the policy, describe what happened and upload photos — most auto and property claims need nothing more. You'll get a claim number, an adjuster assignment and status updates in real time.

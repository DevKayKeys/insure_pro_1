Before you install

Why isn't the app on Google Play or the App Store?

InsurePro installs directly from this page as a progressive web app. You get the same experience — home-screen icon, offline access, push-style alerts — without store accounts, region restrictions or waiting for an approval. The download is signed and served over the same 256-bit encrypted connection as the rest of our site.

Is my policy data available offline?

Yes. Once installed, your declarations pages, digital ID cards and claim history are cached on the device, so you can show proof of insurance with no signal at all. Live actions such as new quotes or filing a claim reconnect automatically when you are back online.

How do I remove the app?

Delete it exactly like any other app: hold the InsurePro icon and choose remove/delete. No account is closed and no data is lost — everything stays in your InsurePro account, and you can reinstall at any time from this page.

Does it work on shared or work phones?

Yes. The app is locked behind your InsurePro credentials or biometrics, auto-locks after 60 seconds idle, and never stores your payment details on the device.
